Available Prediction Features
  • Spending Forecast & Budget Prediction — Predicts your daily spending pattern for the next 30 days and whether you will exceed your spending limit, using a Random Forest Regressor trained on the BudgetWise synthetic dataset (Arfath R, 2024)
  • Top Category Prediction — Predicts which spending category will be highest next month
  • End of Month Balance — Predicts your account balance at end of next month
  • Spending Behaviour Clustering — Classifies your spending behaviour type using K-Means clustering
